# Legal-holiday statute provenance (il-holidays)

### `il-holidays/il-legal-holidays.txt` — 5 ILCS 490 (Illinois State Commemorative Dates Act) and 5 ILCS 70/1.11 (Statute on Statutes, computation of time)

- Source: https://www.ilga.gov/Legislation/ILCS/Articles?ActID=134&ChapterID=2&Chapter=GENERAL%20PROVISIONS&MajorTopic=GOVERNMENT (official Illinois General Assembly ILCS database; the whole Act, which designates the State's holidays) and https://www.ilga.gov/Legislation/ILCS/Articles?ActID=79&ChapterID=2&Chapter=GENERAL%20PROVISIONS&MajorTopic=GOVERNMENT (the Statute on Statutes, of which only § 1.11 is kept). Illinois has no separate legal-holiday statute: 5 ILCS 70/1.11 excludes Saturday, Sunday and 'a holiday as defined or fixed in any statute', and the Commemorative Dates Act is where holidays are fixed (for example Lincoln's Birthday, Columbus Day, Juneteenth National Freedom Day, § 63; a pod must pick the sections that say 'holiday', not the commemorative days and months, and read the observance rule in the Act's Monday/Friday sections).
- Edition: the pages carry no as-of date; the ILCS database notice on the page says recent laws may not yet be included and that statutory changes may appear in it before they take effect; the latest source note in the Act is the highest Public Act number cited in the Act is P.A. 103-279: CURRENCY UNCONFIRMED.
