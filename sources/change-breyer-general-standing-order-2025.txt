## Breyer General Standing Order
Judge Charles R. Breyer, General Standing Order (N.D. Cal., rev. July 16, 2025), the courtesy-copy and scheduling paragraphs as quoted verbatim in the lg-fq2.1 research register (fetched 2026-10-23 from https://cand.uscourts.gov/sites/default/files/standing-orders/General-Standing-Order-Judge-Charles-R.-Breyer-rev-7-16-2025.pdf; a command-line fetch on 2026-10-26 was rejected by the court's firewall).
Courtesy copies. Parties must file a single (no duplicates) courtesy chambers copy of all filings. The courtesy copy must be delivered to the Clerk's Office no later than noon on the court day following the day that the document was electronically filed. (Note: This deadline differs from those in Civil Local Rule 5-1(e)(7)(A).)
No changes in the Court's schedule shall be made except by signed order of the Court and upon a showing of good cause.
