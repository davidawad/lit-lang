                                        Rule 84. Forms

The forms available on the Colorado Judicial Branch Website at
https://www.coloradojudicial.gov/self-help-forms contained in the Appendix to Chapters 1 to
17A are sufficient under the rules and are intended to indicate the simplicity and brevity of
statement which the rules contemplate.
