        The Supreme Court of South Carolina
            Re: Amendment to Appendix H to Part IV, South
            Carolina Appellate Court Rules

            Appellate Case No. 2026-001222



                                     ORDER


The South Carolina Board of Paralegal Certification has requested the Court create
an "Emeritus Status" for South Carolina Certified Paralegals by amending
Appendix H to Part IV of the South Carolina Appellate Court Rules (SCACR).
The request is granted.

Appendix H is amended to add new paragraph IX(G), which provides:

      G. Emeritus Status. To be eligible for Emeritus status, a South
      Carolina Certified Paralegal must (1) be in good standing and at least
      65 years of age or be permanently disabled; (2) have a total of five
      consecutive years as a South Carolina Certified Paralegal; and (3) no
      longer be employed in the legal field. Applications for Emeritus status
      must include a one-time fee of $75. Emeritus members may re-apply
      for active certification should they return to the legal profession.

This amendment is effective immediately.
