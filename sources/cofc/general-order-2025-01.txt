         In the United States Court of Federal Claims
                                    January 2, 2025




                           GENERAL ORDER NO. 2025-01


       The United States Court of Federal Claims will be closed on Thursday, January 9,
2025, in observance of the National Day of Mourning for James Earl Carter, the thirty-
ninth President of the United States.

       For purposes of computation of time under Rule 6 of the Rules of the United
States Court of Federal Claims (RCFC), January 9, 2025, shall be treated in the same
manner as a legal holiday. See RCFC 6(a)(6).

       This order shall be circulated within the court, transmitted to the United States
Court of Federal Claims Bar Association, posted in the Office of the Clerk of Court, and
posted on the court’s website.

      IT IS SO ORDERED.




                                                       ELAINE D. KAPLAN
                                                       Chief Judge
