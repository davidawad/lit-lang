           UNITED STATES COURT OF APPEALS FOR VETERANS CLAIMS


MISC. NO. 09-26

IN RE: IMPLEMENTATION OF THE APPELLATE CASE MANAGEMENT SYSTEM


                                            ORDER

        By direction of the Chief Judge, effective October 1, 2026, new appeals and petitions will
be opened and maintained in the Appellate Case Management System (ACMS). Appeals and
petitions filed before that date will continue to be maintained in the Case Management/Electronic
Case Filing system (CM/ECF).

        Assignment of Cases: Unless otherwise ordered by the Court, every appeal or petition
filed on or after October 1, 2026, shall be opened and maintained in ACMS. Every case submitted
before October 1, 2026, shall continue to be maintained in CM/ECF. The filing date of a case for
purposes of this paragraph shall be determined under Rule 25 of the Court’s Rules of Practice and
Procedure (Rules). The Clerk of the Court may correct the electronic-system assignment of a case;
such a correction does not alter the filing date of the case or any document.

        Subsequent Filings: After a case has been opened, every document required to be filed
electronically must be filed through the electronic filing system in which the case is maintained,
unless the Court’s Rules, the E-Rules, this Order, instructions issued by the Clerk, or an order
entered in the case permits another method of submission.

        Continued Application of Existing Rules: Except as provided in this Order, the Court’s
Rules and the E-Rules remain in effect and apply to cases maintained in ACMS to the same extent
that they apply to cases maintained in CM/ECF.

      Self-Represented Parties: Nothing in this Order eliminates or restricts a method of
submission otherwise permitted by the Court’s Rules or E-Rules for a self-represented party.

       Required Use of the Correct Electronic Filing System: A document required to be filed
through ACMS that is submitted through CM/ECF will not be accepted for filing. A document
required to be filed through CM/ECF that is submitted through ACMS likewise will not be
accepted for filing. The Clerk will direct the filer to resubmit the document through the correct
system. Submission through the incorrect electronic filing system does not constitute filing and
does not preserve or extend a filing deadline. Nothing in this paragraph limits the authority of the
Court to grant relief by order when warranted.

        Required Use of ACMS for Case-Initiated Related Filings: The Court will not accept
case-initiating filings including Notices of Appeal and petitions for extraordinary relief or any
other documents for filing through the esubmission@uscourts.cavc.gov or any electronic mailbox.
Represented filers must initiate new appeals and petitions and submit all other documents through
the ACMS. Pro hoc vice filers are permitted to submit their application and initial notice of
appearance through efiling@uscourts.cavc.gov and all subsequent filings must be submitted
through ACMS.
        Clerk’s Instructions and Conflicting Technical Provisions: The Clerk is authorized to
issue and revise technical instructions, user guides, filing-event lists, registration procedures, and
other administrative guidance necessary to implement this Order and operate ACMS and CM/ECF
during the transition period. When a CM/ECF-specific technical procedure cannot be performed
in ACMS in the same manner, filers must follow the corresponding ACMS procedure and
instructions issued by the Clerk. Such instructions do not alter a filing deadline or any substantive
requirement unless authorized by the Court’s Rules, the E-Rules, or an order of the Court. To the
extent that a CM/ECF-specific provision of the Court’s Rules or E-Rules conflicts with this Order
concerning the assignment of a case to ACMS or the required use of ACMS, this Order controls.

         Publication and Duration: The Clerk shall publish this Order and appropriate ACMS
filing instructions on the Court’s website. This Order takes effect on October 1, 2026, and remains
in effect until amended or superseded by further order of the Court or by amendments to the
Court’s Rules or E-Rules.
