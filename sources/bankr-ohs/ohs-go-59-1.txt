                        UNITED STATES BANKRUPTCY COURT
                           SOUTHERN DISTRICT OF OHIO


                                                   GENERAL ORDER NO. 59-1
 IN RE

 AMENDING LOCAL BANKRUPTCY
 RULE 5081-1



       Effective July 5, 2023, Local Bankruptcy Rule 5081-1(b) is hereby amended to
state as follows:

       (b) Manner of Payment. Filing fees, administrative fees and other scheduled
       fees and charges are payable electronically or by money order, cashier’s check,
       law firm check, or trustee’s check made payable to “Clerk, United States
       Bankruptcy Court.” Credit card payments by non-debtors are accepted. Cash
       payments and personal checks are not accepted.


         IT IS SO ORDERED.

         Dated: June 9, 2023


                                                 FOR THE COURT




                                                 Chief Judge John E. Hoffman, Jr.
                                                 United States Bankruptcy Court
                                                 Southern District of Ohio

       Effective July 5, 2023, Local Bankruptcy Rule 5081-1(b) is hereby amended to
state as follows:


       (b) Manner of Payment. Payment of Filing fees, administrative fees and other
       scheduled fees and charges are payable electronically may be by cash or by
       money order, cashier’s check, law firm check, or trustee’s check, certified check,
       cashier’s check, or court approved credit card from a party other than a debtor.
       Checks shall be made payable to “Clerk, U. S. Bankruptcy Court”. Credit card
       payments by non-debtors are accepted. All payments shall be in the exact
       amount of such fees and charges. Cash payments and personal checks are not
       accepted.
