Montana Code Annotated 2025

TITLE 46. CRIMINAL PROCEDURE

CHAPTER 13. PRETRIAL MOTIONS

Part 2. Specific Pretrial Motions

Return To Original Place Of Trial

46-13-205. Return to original place of trial. When a trial occurs after a change of place of trial and a retrial is required, the cause must be returned to the county in which the charge was originally properly filed for further proceedings.

History: En. Sec. 175, Ch. 800, L. 1991.
