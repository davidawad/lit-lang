RULE 2-605. OFFERS OF JUDGMENT--HEALTH CARE MALPRACTICE CLAIMS

Currentness
A party to a health care malpractice claim may serve on the adverse party an offer of judgment pursuant to Code, Courts Article, § 3-2A-08A.
Cross reference: With respect to “costs” as used in Code, Courts Article, § 3-2A-08A, see Rule 2-603.
Source: This Rule is new.
