Rule 81.1. Juvenile Emancipation

Currentness
The rules that apply to juvenile emancipation proceedings are located in Part V of the Rules of Procedure for the Juvenile Court.

Credits

Added Sept. 2, 2016, effective Jan. 1, 2017. Amended Dec. 8, 2021, effective July 1, 2022.
