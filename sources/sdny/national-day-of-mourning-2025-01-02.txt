         The United States District Court for the Southern District of New York will be closed on
Thursday, January 9, 2025, in observance of the National Day of Mourning for James Earl Carter, Jr., the
thirty-ninth President of the United States.

         For purposes of computation of time under Rule 6 of the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure and
Rule 45 of the Federal Rules of Criminal Procedure, January 9, 2025, shall be treated in the same manner
as a legal holiday. See Fed. R. Civ. P. 6(a)(6); Fed. R. Crim. P. 45(a)(6).

         This order shall be circulated within the court, transmitted to the United States Attorney’s Office
for the Southern District of New York and the Federal Defenders of New York, posted in the Office of the
Clerk of Court, and posted on the court’s website.

Dated: January 2, 2025
       New York, New York
