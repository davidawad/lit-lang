                         UNITED STATES BANKRUPTCY COURT
                             DISTRICT OF RHODE ISLAND



                                 GENERAL ORDER 2025-002


                                  AMENDING LOCAL RULES


Pursuant to 28 U.S.C. § 2071, Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 83, Federal Rule of Bankruptcy
Procedure 9029, and U.S. District Court for the District of Rhode Island General Local Rule
109(g)(l), the following Local Bankruptcy Rules are hereby amended effective July 21, 2025.


                                   Local Rule 1006-1 (amended)
                                   Local Rule 5005-4 (amended)
                                   Local Rule 5079-1 (amended)




The above-referenced local rules, which take effect on July 21, 2025, shall apply to all cases both
filed or pending on or after this date.




IT IS SO ORDERED.


Date: July 21, 2025
