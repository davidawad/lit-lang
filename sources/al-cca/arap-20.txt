                     Alabama Rules of Appellate Procedure

                                      Rule 20.

                        Assignment of error not required.

      Assignments of error are not required. Statement of the issues presented
on appeal in the appellant’s brief as required by Rule 28 shall be sufficient.


                              Committee Comments

         Rule 20 states in positive terms that assignments of error are no longer
required. While perhaps unnecessary, inclusion of this statement emphasizes the
spirit in which these rules are intended to operate: to reach the merits of litigation.
