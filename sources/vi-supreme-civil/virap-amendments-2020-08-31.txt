                                   ORDER OF THE COURT

       THIS MATTER is before the Court for the purpose of adopting the Interim Virgin Islands

Electronic Filing Rules, which shall apply to all proceedings in both the Superior Court and the

Supreme Court which are subject to mandatory electronic filing. Accordingly, it is hereby

       ORDERED that the Interim Virgin Islands Electronic Filing Rules, attached as "Exhibit

1" to this Order, are HEREBY ADOPTED and SHALL GO INTO EFFECT on August 31,

2020. Effective that date, these rules shall apply to all proceedings in the Supreme Court, as well

as all civil, family, small claims, probate, and other non-criminal proceedings in the Superior

Court. They shall also apply to criminal proceedings in the Superior Court effective on the date

electronic filing in such matters becomes mandatory. It is further

       ORDERED that effective August 31, 2020, Rule 40 of the Virgin Islands Rules of

Appellate Procedure SHALL BE REPEALED. It is further

       ORDERED that the Advisory Committee on Rules SHALL REVIEW the Interim Virgin

Islands Electronic Filing Rules and issue a recommendation to the Supreme Court as to whether

they should become permanent, with or without modification. It is further

       ORDERED that copies of this order be directed to the appropriate parties.

       SO ORDERED this      day of August, 2020.
