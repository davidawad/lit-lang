      Order Adopting Comment to Texas Rule of Evidence 404

     ════════════════════════════════════════════════════


ORDERED that:

1.     The Court adopts the following comment to Texas Rule of Evidence 404,
       effective immediately.

2.     The Clerk is directed to:

       a.    file a copy of this order with the Secretary of State;

       b.    send a copy of this order to the Governor, the Lieutenant Governor, and
             each elected member of the Legislature; and

       c.    cause a copy of this order to be mailed to each registered member of the
             State Bar of Texas by publication in the Texas Bar Journal.

       d.    submit a copy of this order for publication in the Texas Register.


Dated: July 2, 2026.
