## USPTO operating status
Major events affecting customers (www.uspto.gov/learning-and-resources/operating-status): the entries designating a day the Office was closed a federal holiday.
Closing of the USPTO on January 26 and 27, 2026
Federal government offices in the Washington, D.C. metropolitan area, including the United States Patent and Trademark Office (USPTO), were officially closed due to weather on Monday, January 26, and Tuesday, January 27, 2026. The USPTO considers those dates federal holidays.
Closing of the USPTO on December 24 and 26, 2025
Federal government offices in the Washington, D.C. metropolitan area, including the United States Patent and Trademark Office (USPTO), were officially closed by executive order on Wednesday, December 24, and Friday, December 26, 2025. The USPTO considers those dates federal holidays. Since Thursday, December 25 was also a holiday, the USPTO deems any action or fee due on December 24, 25, or 26 as timely if taken or paid no later than 11:59 p.m. ET on Monday, December 29, 2025, which was the next business day the USPTO was open. For more information, read the Official Gazette Notice.

## USPTO closed December 24, 2020
Due to the closing of federal government offices, including the United States Patent and Trademark Office (USPTO), on Thursday, December 24, the USPTO will consider Thursday, December 24 to be a holiday.
Since Friday, December 25, is also a holiday, the USPTO will consider actions or fees due on Thursday, December 24 or Friday, December 25 to be timely if taken or paid on Monday, December 28, which is the next business day that the USPTO is open.
