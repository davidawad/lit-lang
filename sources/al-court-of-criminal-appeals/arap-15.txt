                    Alabama Rules of Appellate Procedure

                                     Rule 15.

                                Overruling cases.

      No former adjudication of the court shall be overruled or materially modified
except upon consultation of the court as a whole.


                             Committee Comments

       Rule 15 derives from 1940 Title 13, § 12 and Code of Ala., § 12-2-13. The
statutory requirement that former adjudications of the court, while they can be
overruled by the court, shall not be so overruled without consideration by the court
as a whole is simply carried forward in this rule. This requirement is intended to
eliminate the possibility of a conflict between the rulings of divisions.
