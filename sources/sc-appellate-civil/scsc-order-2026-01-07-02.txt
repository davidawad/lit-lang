        The Supreme Court of South Carolina
             Re: Amendment to Rule 410, South Carolina Appellate
             Court Rules

             Appellate Case No. 2026-000016



                                      ORDER


Pursuant to the provisions of Article V, Section 4 of the South Carolina
Constitution, the Court has amended Rule 410(h)(1)(E) of the South Carolina
Appellate Court Rules (SCACR), which defines the military member class of bar
membership, as set forth in the attachment to this order.

The amendment removes the prohibition on military members engaging in the
practice of law in South Carolina outside of their duties in the Armed Forces of the
United States.

The amendment is effective immediately.

Rule 410(h)(1)(E), SCACR, is amended to provide:

(E) Military Member. Any member who is serving on active duty with the
Armed Forces of the United States for six months or more, including members
of the National Guard and other reserve components, and elects to become a
military member.
