                UNITED STATES COURT OF INTERNATIONAL TRADE




 Closure of the United States Court of
 International Trade to Observe the
                                                              ORDER
 National Day of Mourning for President
 James “Jimmy” Earl Carter, Jr.




      By order of the Chief Judge, the United States Court of International Trade will be
closed on Thursday, January 9, 2025, in observance of the National Day of Mourning for
President James “Jimmy” Earl Carter, Jr.
       For the purposes of the computation of time and motions to enlarge time under
U.S. Court of International Trade Rule 6, January 9, 2025, will be considered a “legal
holiday.”




Dated:     1/2/2025                        /s/ Mark A. Barnett
         New York, New York               Chief Judge Mark A. Barnett
