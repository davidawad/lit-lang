                GENERAL ORDER RESCINDING GENERAL ORDER 08-06
                      AND RESCINDING S.D.W.Va. LBR 3011-1



         This matter is before the Court based on the immediate need to update the Court’s

policies and procedures relating to Applications (formerly required as Motions) for Unclaimed

Funds.

         It is ORDERED that General Order 08-06 be and hereby is RESCINDED.

         FURTHER, it is ORDERED that S.D.W.Va. LBR 3011-1 be and hereby is

RESCINDED.
