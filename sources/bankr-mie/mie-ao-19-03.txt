ADMINISTRATIVE ORDER AMENDING
LOCAL BANKRUPTCY RULE 1073-1(a)(4)

This administrative order is being entered to address the procedure for
reassignment when a judge enters an order for recusal. Presently, E.D. Mich.
LBR 1073-1(a)(4) provides that when a judge enters an order for recusal,
reassignment will be by blind draw to a judge in Detroit. That made sense when
Judge Opperman was the only judge in both the Bay City and Flint locations.
However, now that Judge Applebaum will be sitting in Flint, and Judge Opperman
will be sitting in Bay City, it makes sense to amend the rule to provide for the
reassignment of Bay City matters to Flint, the reassignment of Flint matters either to
Bay City or Detroit, and the reassignment of Detroit matters to other judges in
Detroit. Accordingly,

IT Is HEREBY ORDERED that E.D. Mich. LBR 1073-1(a)(4) is amended to
read as follows:
(4) When a judge enters an order for recusal with respect to a
case, a particular matter arising therein, or an adversary
proceeding, reassignment will be made by the clerk as
follows:
(A) recusals in Bay City will be reassigned to Flint;
(B) recusals in Flint will be reassigned by blind draw to
another judge in Detroit or Bay City; and
(C) recusals in Detroit will be reassigned by blind draw
to another judge in Detroit.
PHILLIP J. SHERFERLY, Chief Judge
