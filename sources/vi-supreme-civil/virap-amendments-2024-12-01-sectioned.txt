                                   ORDER OF THE COURT

       Pursuant to its inherent authority and the authority granted to it by section 21(c) of the

Revised Organic Act of 1954, and title 4 sections 31(a) and 32(1) of the Virgin Islands Code the

Supreme Court of the Virgin Islands proposes the following amendment to the Virgin Islands

Rules of Appellate Procedure Accordingly it is hereby

## Rule 40
       ORDERED that the following language SHALL BE DESIGNATED as Rule 40 of the

Virgin Islands Rules of Appellate Procedure

       Rule 40 Petition for Hearing or Rehearing En Banc, En Banc Determination

       (a) When Hearmg or Rehearing En Banc May Be Ordered A maj ority of the
       justices, who are in regular active service, and who are not disqualiﬁed may order
       that an appeal or other proceedings be heard or reheard en banc A majority of the
       justices shall include any senior sitting justice or designated justice assigned to the
       case An en banc hearing or rehearing is not favored and ordinarily will not be
       ordered unless
               (I) en banc consideration is necessary to secure or maintain uniformity of
       the court’s decisions, or
               (2) the proceeding involves a question of exceptional importance

               court is therefore necessary to secure and maintain uniformity of the court's
               decisions. or
                       (B) the proceeding involves one or more questions of exceptional
               importance, each of which must be concisely stated

## Rule 31
        ORDERED that Rule 3| of the Virgin Islands Rules of Appellate Procedure SHALL BE

AMENDED by striking all existing language in its entirety and replacing it with the following

new language

        Rule 31 Panel Hearing and Rehearing

        (a) Panels Except in cases where the Court has detetmined that a matter should be heard
            en banc, all cases are assigned for consideration to a panel of three Justices
        (b) Composition ofPanels All panels will consist solely of active Justices of the
            Supreme Court unless disqualiﬁcations vacancies illness, prolonged absences
            scheduling conﬂicts, or other relevant consideration requires appointment of
            one or more Senior or Designated Justices to heat a particular case The Chief
            Justice, with the cooperation of the Associate Justices and the assistance of the
                Clerk of the Court, shall develop a rotating schedule for the automatic
            assignment of three Justice panels to hear all cases at a given session of the
            Court, provided, however, that such schedule may be modiﬁed to ensure an
            even distribution of caseloads amongst the Justices The composition of the
            panel hearing a given case shall not be announced prior to issuance of the
            calendar for a given Court session
        (c) Panel Demsions Binding Decisions issued by a panel of the Court are
            precedents of the Court which are binding on all lower courts as well as
            subsequent panels of the Court No panel may overrule or modify a prior
            decision of the Court, whether issued en banc or by another panel, without
            consideration by the Court en banc
        (d) Peltnonsfor Panel Rehearmg
                    (1) Time Unless the time is shortened or extended by order, a petition
                for rehearing by the panel may be filed within 14 days aﬂer entry of
               judgment
                    (2) Contents The petition must state with particularity each point of law
                or fact that the petitioner believes the panel has overlooked or
                misapprehended and must argue in support of the petition Oral argument
                in support of the petition is not permitted
                    (3) Response Unless the panel requests, no reSponse to a petition for
                rehearing by the panel and no reply to a response may be ﬁled
