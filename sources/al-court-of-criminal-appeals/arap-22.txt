                    Alabama Rules of Appellate Procedure

                                     Rule 22.

                          Appeals in habeas corpus.

       Appeals in habeas corpus proceedings shall be governed by Code of
Alabama, § 12-22-90; except that habeas corpus appeals in child-custody cases
shall be governed by provisions of these rules that govern appeals in civil cases.

[Amended 5-7-2015, eff. 8-1-2015.]


                             Committee Comments

        This rule basically continues Alabama practice under Code of Ala., § 1222-
90. It does resolve a question as to where habeas corpus appeals in child custody
go by providing that such appeals are governed by the various provisions of these
rules governing appeals in civil cases. As such, appeal would lie to the Court of
Civil Appeals.
