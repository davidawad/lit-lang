General Order 26-02



                                    UNITED STATES DISTRICT COURT
                                     EASTERN DISTRICT OF TEXAS

                            GENERAL ORDER AMENDING LOCAL RULES

           In light of commentary received regarding General Order 25-07, it is hereby ORDERED
that the following amendments to Local Rules CV-4(a), CV-79(a)(4), and CR-55(c), having been
approved by the district judges of this court, are adopted for immediate implementation.1

LOCAL RULE CV-4

(a)        ***

           If service of summons is not waived, the plaintiff must prepare and submit a summons to the
           clerk for each defendant to be served with a copy of the complaint. Unless exempt from
           electronic filing under Local Rule CV-5(a), the plaintiff must submit the summons by
           uploading it to the Electronic Filing System using the Request for Clerk to Issue Document
           event.

COMMENT: The exemption from the requirement for electronic filing afforded to pro se
litigants in Local Rule CV-5(a) is now recognized in this rule.


LOCAL RULES CV-79(a)(4) and CR-55(c)

           Exhibits in the court’s custody outside of the court’s Electronic Filing System that are not
           docketed as part of the case file need only be retained through the final disposition of the
           case as defined by the schedule on the court’s website. Thereafter, they may be disposed of
           by the clerk in accordance with the retention provisions available on the court’s website.
           The party offering any exhibit shall be responsible for its removal within 30 days after final
           disposition of the case. Any exhibit remaining more than 30 days after final disposition of
           the case may be disposed of by the clerk.

COMMENT: Language in both rules is now more consistent with phrasing and guidance provided
by the Judiciary Records Officers of the Administrative Office of the U.S. Courts.
