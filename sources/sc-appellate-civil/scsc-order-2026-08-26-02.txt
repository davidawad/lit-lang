        The Supreme Court of South Carolina
            Re: South Carolina Appellate Court Rules

            Appellate Case No. 2026-001461


                                    ORDER


This order amends several rules in the South Carolina Appellate Court Rules
(SCACR). The amendments, which are set forth below, correct minor scrivener's
errors or make non-substantive changes to several rules.

   • Rules 401 and 402, SCACR, are amended so that both rules refer to the
     Joseph F. Rice School of Law.
   • Comment 3 to Rule 4.2, RPC, Rule 407, SCACR, is amended to correct a
     scrivener's error.
   • Rule 7.3(d)(2) and (3), RPC, Rule 407, SCACR, is amended to update phone
     and address contact requirements for lawyer solicitations.

These changes are effective immediately.
