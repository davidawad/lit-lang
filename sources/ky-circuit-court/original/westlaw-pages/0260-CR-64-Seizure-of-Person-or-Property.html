Kentucky Rules of Civil Procedure (CR) Rule 64

CR 64 Seizure of person or property

Currentness
At the commencement of and during the course of an action, all remedies providing for seizure of person or property for the purpose of securing satisfaction of the judgment ultimately to be entered in the action are available under the circumstances and in the manner provided by law.
