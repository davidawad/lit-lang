                                  STANDING ORDER 2025-1

                        OBJECTION DEADLINE FOR MOTIONS
                     SET FOR HEARING ON MONDAYS AT 2:00 P.M.

## SO 2025-1 Rule 9014-1
       Notwithstanding Local Rule 9014-1(a)(1), which requires objections to be filed eight

days prior to a scheduled hearing, and considering further F.R.B.P. 9006(a)(1)(C), which

provides that “if the last day is a Saturday, Sunday, or legal holiday, the period continues to run

until the end of the next day”:

       IT IS ORDERED that for all hearings scheduled on Mondays at 2:00 p.m., the deadline

to file objections or responses to any such hearing shall be 11:59 p.m. on the prior Monday.

       Baton Rouge, Louisiana, November 25, 2025.
