ADMINISTRATIVE ORDER
ABROGATING LOCAL BANKRUPTCY RULE 9037-1

The Court is entering this administrative order to abrogate Local Bankruptcy
Rule 9037-1 because it is now in conflict with the Federal Rules of Bankruptcy
Procedure.

On December 1, 2019, Fed. R. Bankr. P. 9037(h) was amended to set forth a
procedure for the filing of a motion to redact a previously filed document that contains
personal identification information. Effective immediately, Local Bankruptcy
Rule 9037-1 is abrogated and the procedure in Fed. R. Bankr. P. 9037(h) is the sole
procedure that may be used to file a motion to redact a previously filed document.

IT Is SO ORDERED.
