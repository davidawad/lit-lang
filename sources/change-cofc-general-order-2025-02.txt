## COFC General Order 2025-02
United States Court of Federal Claims, General Order No. 2025-02 (Oct. 6, 2025), the operative text as quoted verbatim in the lg-fq2.1 research register (fetched 2026-10-23 from https://www.uscfc.uscourts.gov/sites/cfc/files/general-orders/2025.10.06%20General%20Order%20re%202025%20day%20after%20Thanksgiving.pdf; a command-line fetch on 2026-10-26 was rejected by the court's firewall).
The United States Court of Federal Claims will be closed on Friday, November 28, 2025. For purposes of computation of time under Rule 6 of the Rules of the United States Court of Federal Claims (RCFC), November 28, 2025, shall be treated in the same manner as a legal holiday. See RCFC 6(a)(6).
