                          UNITED STATES BANKRUPTCY COURT
                              DISTRICT OF RHODE ISLAND



                                GENERAL ORDER 2026-001


                     AMENDING LOCAL RULES AND APPENDIX III


Pursuant to 28 U.S.C. § 2071, Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 83, Federal Rule of Bankruptcy
Procedure 9029, and U.S. District Court for the District of Rhode Island General Local Rule
109(g)(l), the following Local Bankruptcy Rules and Appendix are hereby amended effective
February 13, 2026.


                                Local Rule 1005-1 (amended)
                                Local Rule 5005-4 (amended)
                                Local Rule 5701-1 (amended)

                                   Appendix III (amended)

The above-referenced amendments, which take effect on February 13, 2026, shall apply to all
cases both filed or pending on or after this date.




IT IS SO ORDERED.
