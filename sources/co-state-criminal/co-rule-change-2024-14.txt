COLORADO RULES OF CRIMINAL PROCEDURE

Where the offense charged is a misdemeanor or petty offense, the action may be commenced in
the county court as provided below in this Rule. This Rule shall have no application to
misdemeanors or petty offenses prosecuted in other courts or to felonies.
