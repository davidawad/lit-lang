                         UNITED STATES BANKRUPTCY COURT
                             DISTRICT OF RHODE ISLAND



                                GENERAL ORDER 2025-003


                         AMENDING LOCAL RULES AND FORMS


Pursuant to 28 U.S.C. § 2071, Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 83, Federal Rule of Bankruptcy
Procedure 9029, and U.S. District Court for the District of Rhode Island General Local Rule
109(g)(l), the following Local Bankruptcy Rules and Forms are hereby amended effective
December 1, 2025.


                                Local Rule 1005-1 (amended)
                                Local Rule 3002-1 (amended)
                                Local Rule 3016-1 (amended)

                                 Local Form 3016-1.1 (new)
                                Local Form 7026-1 (amended)

The above-referenced local rules and forms, which take effect on December 1, 2025, shall apply
to all cases both filed or pending on or after this date.




IT IS SO ORDERED.


Date: December 1, 2025
